# Clear Day Family

Skills for parents and guardians who use Clear Day Family. They work with the signed-in Clear Day Family connector, which reads only your own family's plans and logs.

## What you can ask

- What's coming up for the family this week, with the email each plan came from.
- What is on a child's school schedule today (specials, early dismissal, special days from your plans).
- How a child's day is going, from the feeds, diapers and sleep you and your caregivers logged.
- Add a reminder, or mark an item done. The assistant asks you before it changes anything.

## Connecting

Add the connector in your assistant's connector settings, then sign in with the email you use for Clear Day Family. Clear Day emails you a 6-digit code. A consent screen lists what the assistant can and can't see before you approve. You can disconnect at any time in the Clear Day Family app, or by removing the connector in your assistant.

## What is never available

Photos, log notes, health or medical details, school records, and anything about another family. Nothing can be deleted, shared or sent through this connector.
